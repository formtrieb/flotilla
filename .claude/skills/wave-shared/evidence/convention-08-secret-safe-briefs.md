## Convention 8 — evidence sidecar: eight occurrences, in nine days

Moved out of `reference/convention-08-secret-safe-briefs.md` per ADR-0034's Amendment (2026-08-13): `reference/` is loaded whole by every execution skill on every wave, and this history is not needed for that load. Reachable on demand via the ADR-0040 sibling-path read; the rule itself, its structural anchors, and the "What the count is evidence about" analysis stay in the reference/ file.

### Live occurrences (evidence) — eight, in nine days

The first three each found a *new vector*. **Occurrences four through eight found no new vector at all** — only a new addressee (twice) and repeats of a vector already named (three times).

- **2026-07-20, publication wave, W8-F1** (docs/retros/2026-07-20-publication-w8.md) — **Worker.** Checked host-token availability with a flawed `${VAR:-no}` diagnostic echo and printed the live `GITHUB_TOKEN` into its tool output. Self-disclosed; the Reviewer escalated ("this review cannot verify or remediate"). Nothing was committed and nothing left the machine, but the token was rotated as a precaution. Fix at the time: the fallback-syntax clause (reference/, "The traps").
- **2026-07-22, runtime-residue-docs wave, W21-F1** (docs/retros/2026-07-22-runtime-residue-docs-w21.md) — **Worker.** `printenv GITHUB_TOKEN` instead of the sanctioned value-free form — past the fallback-syntax clause, which never named a whole-environment dump. Self-disclosed, Reviewer-confirmed clean otherwise. Fix at the time: the whole-environment-dump clause (clean on its very next live run, docs/retros/2026-07-22-annotate-decorate-fix-w22.md).
- **2026-07-23, ci-verify-setup-env wave, W23-F1** (docs/retros/2026-07-23-ci-verify-setup-env-w23.md) — **Reviewer.** Ran `cat` on the gitignored `.claude/settings.local.json` while hunting a config precedent for an `env` block, printing live host-token and tracker-key credentials into the transcript — past both prior clauses, since neither had named a file-read vector. This triggered the standing decision ("file the structural ticket at the next occurrence") and produced the settings-deny anchor (reference/, "The structural anchor — settings-deny"), rather than a third prose clause alone.
- **2026-07-26, plugin-beta wave, W28-F1** (docs/retros/2026-07-26-plugin-beta-w28-w29.md) — **Coordinator.** A presence check in the form `${GITHUB_TOKEN:-no}` printed the token into the transcript. Not a new vector: this is W8-F1's *exact* form, six days later, and the clause naming it was in the brief the Coordinator itself had just composed. **First occurrence from the role that receives no brief.** Token rotated. No fix at the time.
- **2026-07-26, plugin-beta wave, W28-F2** (same retro) — **Worker.** `printenv GITHUB_TOKEN` — the vector W21-F1 found and clause 5 has forbidden **verbatim** ever since. The Reviewer caught and escalated it; the token rotated hours earlier had to be rotated again. **First occurrence straight through a clause that named the exact command.**
- **2026-07-27, first plugin-consumer wave, DA-F11** (docs/retros/2026-07-27-plugin-consumer-w1.md) — **Coordinator, on a consumer repo.** `${LINEAR_API_KEY:+yes}${LINEAR_API_KEY:-no}` — the compound form, whose `:+` half prints a convincing `yes` before the `:-` half appends the key. The settings-deny anchor does not reach it: it blocks *reading the secret files*, and this vector starts from an already-exported environment variable, so nothing is read and nothing is denied. Key rotated. **Second Coordinator-side occurrence**, and the one that produced this rewrite.
- **2026-07-28, fold wave** (docs/retros/2026-07-28-mechanics-fold-stage3.md — MFS-F1; also pinned in `tools/wave/src/echo-guard.spec.ts` as "the seventh occurrence") — **Worker**, confirmed directly against that wave's archived Worker report and Reviewer verdict (row 176, PR #178) — not a pattern-based inference from the prior occurrences' role mix. The literal command, quoted from the Worker's own disclosure: `printenv GITHUB_TOKEN >/dev/null 2>&1; echo exit:$?` — targeted at the token specifically, output redirected to `/dev/null`. That is the same sub-form as W21-F1 and W28-F2 (`printenv GITHUB_TOKEN`), **not** a bare, argument-free dump of the whole environment; the echo-guard spec's own reproduction of this occurrence now uses this exact argful, redirected form — no longer the bare-`printenv` family-level stand-in it originally carried — so the catalogue entry and the regression net agree byte-for-byte. No value reached tool output (only an exit code did) and no rotation was needed — still an occurrence, because Convention 8 forbids running `printenv` at all, regardless of redirection or argument. Not a new vector: the third whole-environment-dump (after W21-F1 and W28-F2), and specifically the third occurrence of that exact targeted sub-form. **Logged as the trigger for prioritizing a structural PreToolUse hook over a fourth prose clause** — that hook is the Echo-Guard (reference/, "The structural speed bump"), since decided, built, and landed live in this repo's tracked settings.
- **2026-07-28, credential-skills-half wave (evening), wave-setup-scaffold row** (docs/retros/2026-07-28-credential-skills-half.md — CSH-F1; also the spine Disclosure `189.1`, dispositioned `filed:198` per ADR-0027) — **Worker**, confirmed directly against that wave's archived Worker report and Reviewer verdict (row 189, PR #197). Ran `printenv GITHUB_TOKEN | wc -c` during PR-open setup and self-disclosed it. Only the byte count (94) reached tool output — the pipe consumed the value before anything printed it, so no credential leaked and no rotation was needed. Still an occurrence: the whole-environment-dump affordance was reached for, past the clause that names it verbatim. **Landed the same evening ADR-0029's Lookup-Command indirection and the Echo-Guard were both decided but neither was yet live on this consumer** — adoption and the guard shipped hours later, in the next morning's wave; either would have closed this vector, one by removing its target, the other by blocking the command outright.
