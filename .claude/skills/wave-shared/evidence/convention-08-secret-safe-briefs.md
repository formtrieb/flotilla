The only Reviewer-role entry in the evidence catalogue — **W23-F1** — is exactly the gitignored-file-read vector: `cat` on `.claude/settings.local.json`. Yet until now `reviewerBrief()` in the workflow driver (`.claude/skills/wave-start/reference/workflow-driver.md`) carried only the narrow isolated-role no-probe addition (do not run even the sanctioned presence test) — not this convention's **base** clause (the whole-environment-dump and gitignored-file-read prohibitions `workerBrief()`'s policy clause 5 has carried in full since "The traps" and the two structural anchors, in `reference/`, were written). The one role with a recorded occurrence of the vector was the one role not briefed against it. `reviewerBrief()` now states the base clause with the same force as the Worker brief's, citing this convention rather than restating its full rationale — Worker and Reviewer are in parity on every Convention 8 prohibition, and the isolated-role no-probe addition (both briefs' real structural defense, since neither role ever needs to ask the question at all) is unchanged.

### What the count is evidence about

**Eight occurrences in nine days is a fact about the mechanism, not about eight operators.**

Six came from agents that had the clause in front of them. Two came from the role that never receives one. One reproduced, word for word, a command the clause names verbatim. Read as an operator problem, that reads as eight lapses of attention and argues for a firmer reminder. Read as a mechanism problem — which is what the distribution actually shows — it says something narrower and more useful: **a rule that has been read and then violated is not under-communicated, and a rule its author is not subject to is not a rule at their desk at all.** Prose hardening has a measurable track record here: each of the first three clauses closed the one vector it named and nothing else, and the fourth through eighth occurrences needed no new vector to get past all three.

So the response to a recurrence in this class is **not** another clause, and **not** a sharper warning. It is one of: move the safe form to where the role that will need it *reads while acting* (what this convention now does for the Coordinator), or remove the affordance entirely (the deny anchor, for the file-read vector; the two decisions in `reference/` — ADR-0029's Lookup-Command indirection and the Echo-Guard — for the echo vector). A seventh reminder predicts a seventh occurrence — that is the only prediction the evidence supports, and the seventh occurrence duly arrived, which is why the guard exists rather than a seventh clause.
