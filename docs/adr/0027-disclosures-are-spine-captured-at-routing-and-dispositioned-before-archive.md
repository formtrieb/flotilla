# Disclosures are spine-captured at routing and dispositioned before archive

Convention 9/10 make agents *disclose* gaps (a shipped capability whose call site lies outside the slice's declared Files, a surviving runtime resource), and the detection demonstrably works — on 2026-07-27 seven wiring gaps were disclosed by their Workers and confirmed by their Reviewers. What did not exist was anything that turns a detected gap into a durable artifact: whether a disclosure became a follow-up depended on a human's memory at the end of the day, and one of the seven (Gate 8 shipped inert — `runDor`/`runDorById` never thread the verify config) was still unfiled when this was decided. The knowledge exists at verdict-routing and evaporated before close. Decision: capture at routing, enforce at archive — existence mechanically, quality humanly.

## Decision

- **The Spine gains a Disclosures section.** At verdict-routing (`wave-start` step 7) the Coordinator captures every disclosure as a spine entry — source-neutral: Worker prose (Convention 9/10 `judgmentCalls`), Reviewer verdict, or the Coordinator's own observation land identically. Written through paired engine verbs (`spine add-disclosure` / `spine set-disposition`, printer paired with parser — the ADR-0016 principle); never hand-formatted, never grep-parsed.
- **Dispositions:** `open` (default at capture) → `resolved-in-slice · scope-extension · filed:<id> · dropped:<reason>`. Deliberately the row-disposition vocabulary (park, abandon): a scripted, human-decided exit — never automatic.
- **`wave-close` gains a fail-closed checked step before Archive:** an engine check lists `open` disclosures; any hit blocks the archive. The gate demands *that* a disposition exists, never judges its quality — `dropped:<reason>` passes, keeping the human override cheap and explicit.
- **`filed:` means a bare issue,** created inline by the Coordinator via `issue-store create` at disposition time: title, gap description, provenance line (wave slug, row id, iteration) — deliberately **without** an Eligibility label. Wave-readiness comes later through `to-issues` decorate-mode, with the next wave's planning context (a wiring-gap follow-up's Files are precisely the call sites outside the old slice — that wants the bias-toward-wider lens, not a 1 a.m. close ceremony). Existence and readiness are separate steps, as the tracker already models (`#112` is deliberately not `ready-for-agent`).
- **The disclosure channel is unchanged.** Convention 9/10 stay brief-level prose (`judgmentCalls` mirrored in `reviewerFocusItems`); no agent-boundary schema change.

## Considered Options

- **Auto-file every disclosure** (rejected) — one of the seven resolved in-slice (a ticket would be noise); disclosures map N:1 onto issues (DA-F5 + DA-F6 → one ticket); and a mechanically-filed issue has no Header-Block quality yet *looks* handled — the inverse failure of "ein Fund ohne Nummer ist nicht abgelegt".
- **Named-but-unenforced Coordinator duty** (rejected) — the status quo that failed seven times in one day; a duty without an enforcement point evaporates exactly when the day was long.
- **Structured `wiringGaps[]` at the agent boundary** (rejected) — capture-from-prose went 7/7; the observed hole is retention + enforcement, not recognition (Convention 9 itself records that this class is not catchable at the schema boundary). The agent-boundary schema is also the surface every plugin consumer copies and must resync (the DA-F1 driver-drift cost), while the spine has zero consumer contact; and covering the Reviewer as a source would have required a second schema change.
- **Skill-side markdown convention, `wave-close` greps** (rejected) — the #141 class: a convention-coupled spine parse-back already broke once and was fixed by routing through the real reader (#146). A counting gate measures via an engine verb's exit code; it does not read prose.
- **Full `to-issues` slicing at close** (rejected) — demands the highest-judgment work at the structurally worst hour, which is the exact failure condition being fixed.

## Consequences

- Engine: Disclosures section in `renderSpine`, the two verbs plus the engine-owned reader, specs for the write→read round-trip and the close-gate check.
- **The bare-file path's engine accommodation is resolved:** `issue-store create`'s `CreateInput` Header-Block fields (`risk`, `worker`, `files`, `blockedBy`, `acceptanceCriteria`) are now optional **as a group** — all five present (decorated) or all five absent (bare), never a partial set; `classifyCreateInput` rejects a half-written Header-Block and a bare input carrying a decoration-only stowaway (`unblocks`/`parent`/`estimatedWallclock`), both as a usage error, before any write. `filed:` mints through `issue-store create`'s bare mode. The raw host-CLI interim path this ADR left open at build time is retired — bare filing goes through the engine seam like every other write.
- `wave-start` step 7 gains the capture duty; `wave-close` gains the checked step before Archive; `wave-shared` Conventions 9/10 gain one pointer sentence ("routing captures this into the spine").
- Extends the ADR-0002 WAL doctrine to disclosures: written at the moment of knowledge, enforced at the terminal boundary — a Coordinator death between routing and close loses nothing.
- Amends [ADR-0018](0018-wave-execution-runs-on-a-single-workflow-driver-with-a-shared-skill.md) only on the Coordinator side (routing + close); the driver pipeline and both agent schemas are untouched.

## Amendment 2026-07-31 — Disposition defaults: triviality threshold, thematic bundles, observation-not-prescription

The original decision solved retention and promptly overshot: with every disclosure durable, the default disposition drifted toward one `filed:` per disclosure. Measured on 2026-07-30 — one wave closed 9 issues and filed 10 new ones (~1:1 replacement, 100 % meta-work), while the same day's bundled close turned 13 disclosures into 4 issues (#323–#326). Two of the day's tickets were misfiled: #307's premise was already false on main at filing, #303 filed a wrong causal diagnosis that was faithfully implemented and flipped twice. This amendment adds **disposition-quality defaults**; the archive gate itself is untouched — it still demands *that* a disposition exists and never judges its quality.

- **Triviality default (a predicate, not a class list):** a disclosure earns its **own** ticket only when it names a **mechanism defect with an observed consequence** — something that, unfixed, would change the behaviour of an agent, guard, or gate in a future wave. Everything else (measurement residue, dead code, doc staleness, catalog upkeep) defaults into a bundle. The Coordinator may **promote** on judgment (a constructed repro à la #326 qualifies via judgment, not automatically); evidence may come from any spine source (Worker prose, Reviewer confirmation, Coordinator observation). A security-relevant guard hole needs no special-case rule: a detection gap *is* a mechanism defect with observed consequence.
- **Bundles are thematic, never per-wave.** A thematic bundle (#324: doc-reconciliation; #323: HITL-gate follow-ups) has coherent Files, one Risk, one Worker — it decorates into a single grabbable row. A per-wave hygiene bundle mixes whatever the wave stirred up: its Files-scope becomes a conflict-map hub and fails "files-scope sound" structurally, trading parallelism for close-time convenience.
- **`filed:<id>` N:1 is the whole bundling mechanism.** No `bundled:` token, no bundle id — several disclosures sharing one filed id *are* the bundle (the original decision's own reasoning already sanctioned N:1). New vocabulary would touch engine validation, specs, and the glossary without adding a bit of information.
- **A bundle accepts appends only while bare.** A still-bare same-theme bundle from an earlier wave takes further items (`filed:<existing-id>` + amend with a provenance line, ADR-0025-conformant). Once decorated, its scope is frozen — file a fresh bundle; never mutate the Files declaration of an issue that may already sit in a conflict map or claim.
- **`filed:` authors the observation, never the unverified diagnosis** (the #303 rule): symptom + evidence + provenance in the body; a causal claim or fix prescription only when verified, otherwise explicitly marked as hypothesis. The later Worker investigates fresh instead of implementing a 1 a.m. diagnosis.
- **Premise currency is checked at triage, not at close** (the #307 rule): for any issue carrying a wave-provenance line, the `triage` skill verifies the premise against current main **regardless of category** (closing the repro step's bugs-only hole) before recommending; `superseded`-close is the cheap exit. This names the previously missing check on the bare→ready transition — the bare lifecycle stage this ADR introduced had no verification step at all. `to-issues` decorate stays untouched (triage work is out of its boundary by its own rule).

Rejected: **a verify-before-file gate at close** — highest-judgment work at the structurally worst hour (this ADR's own founding argument), and it would not have caught #303: the misdiagnosis survived multiple live looks and fell only when the fix was exercised. Cause-attribution errors are caught by fresh investigation, which the observation-not-prescription rule preserves and a close-time check would not provide.

Consequences: skill-guidance changes only (`wave-close` disposition step, `triage` premise-currency check, `to-issues` untouched); zero engine/schema surface, so the defaults travel to consumers as plugin text and can be overridden by taste — a consumer who prefers 1:1 filing just files 1:1; the gate is satisfied either way. Related cost measure decided the same day: `wave-plan` gains a dispatch-cost estimate line per candidate set (rows × ~4 agents, heavy count) — the budget decision stays human at planning time; deliberately **no** wave-cadence rule (cadence is pool-shaped: the conflict map dictates the largest safe set, and the day's cost driver was row count, not wave count).
